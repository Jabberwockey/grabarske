Since 1997 I'm a LARPer, a hobby, that lets you dive into other worlds and where
you take pleasure in building these worlds from the ground up. One of my on-going
projects is the scriptorium, an in-game publishing house of books in that world.

For several years I published the Aldradach Almanac, a book about the town on the
Drachenfest, the second-biggest LARP event in Germany.

But I also maintain other publications.

Here's the link: [Aldradacher Skriptorium](https://www.aldradacher-skriptorium.de/)
